# Tables

Define the table's information purpose, reader, units, sorting, totals, and
normal viewing distance. Aggregate before rendering; do not place arbitrary
dataframe dumps on slides.

Limit rows/columns to readable content, align numbers consistently, distinguish
totals sparingly, and avoid decorative complexity. If content must paginate,
preserve column widths, headers, number formats, and ordering across slides.

If the minimum font or available area cannot remain legible, move the detail to
an appendix, workbook, attachment, or another artifact rather than silently
shrinking it.
