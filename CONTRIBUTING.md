# Contributing

Contributions are accepted under the repository's MIT License.

Submit only material that you wrote or have the right to contribute under MIT.
Do not copy API documentation, examples, source code, tests, Agent Skills, or
generated material whose license or provenance is unknown. Public facts and API
names may be described in original language; keep quotations short, necessary,
attributed, and compatible with their source terms.

When adapting third-party material:

1. record its URL, author or project, license, and the files affected;
2. preserve all required copyright, license, attribution, NOTICE, and source
   obligations;
3. add or update `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` in the same change; and
4. do not imply endorsement or ownership of third-party names or trademarks.

Generated contributions must be reviewed for copied text, private information,
credentials, and incompatible licensing before submission.
